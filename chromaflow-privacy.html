ChromaFlow – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 May 2026

ChromaFlow is a puzzle game developed by Muhammad Asif, Aarhus, Denmark. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

ChromaFlow does not collect, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information. All game progress (completed levels, move counts, stars) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage (Hive) and is never uploaded to any server.

Third-Party Services

ChromaFlow does not integrate any analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or third-party tracking libraries. No data leaves your device.

Permissions

The app requests no special Android permissions beyond those granted automatically to all apps. Audio playback uses on-device assets only.

Children

ChromaFlow is suitable for all ages. Because no personal data is collected, the app is compliant with COPPA and similar children's privacy regulations by design.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any revision.
