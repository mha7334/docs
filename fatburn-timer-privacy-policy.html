Fat Burn Timer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Fat Burn Timer is an interval workout timer developed by Muhammad Asif, Aarhus, Denmark (package: dk.masif.fatburntimer). This policy explains what data the app processes and how it is handled.

What Fat Burn Timer Does

Fat Burn Timer plays alternating beeps at two intervals you choose, for a session length you pick, and shows a countdown while the session runs. That is the whole app. It works entirely offline.

Data Collection

Fat Burn Timer collects no personal data whatsoever. Specifically, the app does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, or any account information — the app has no accounts or sign-in
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, files, or any other content on your device
  • Health or fitness measurements — the app records no workouts and keeps no history
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics

The interval and session-length values you type are held in memory while the app is open and are gone when it closes. Nothing is written to a database or a server.

Internet Access

Fat Burn Timer does not request the INTERNET permission and makes no network connections of any kind. The beep tones are generated on your device mathematically, not downloaded.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. The only device capabilities it uses are:

  • Audio playback – to sound the interval beeps through your speaker or headphones
  • Keep screen awake – to stop the display sleeping mid-session, active only while a session is running

Neither requires your approval, and neither is used for advertising, tracking, or analytics.

Third-Party Services

Fat Burn Timer contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics libraries, and no crash-reporting services. No third party receives any data from this app.

Data Sharing

There is no data to share. Fat Burn Timer does not sell, rent, or transmit information to anyone.

Data Deletion

Because nothing is stored, there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the app removes it completely from your device.

Children

Fat Burn Timer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app collects no data at all, no personal information from any user is ever gathered.

Health Disclaimer

Fat Burn Timer is a general-purpose interval timer, not a medical or diagnostic device. It provides no medical advice and makes no measurements. Consult a qualified professional before starting any exercise programme.
