Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Muhammad Asif ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Quran Urdu + mobile application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

Location Data

The App requests access to your device's location (GPS) for the following purposes only:

  • Calculating accurate daily prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) for your location
  • Determining the Qibla direction (compass bearing toward the Kaaba in Mecca)

Location data is used only in real time to perform these calculations. It is never stored on our servers, never logged, and never shared with any third party.

No Other Data Collected

The App does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identity information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Audio recordings or microphone data
  • Contacts, messages, or any other device data

2. How We Use Information

Location data is processed entirely on-device solely to compute prayer times and Qibla bearing. No data leaves your device for our purposes.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user data with third parties.

The App streams Quran audio from TrueMuslims.net when a Surah has not been downloaded locally. In this case your device makes a standard HTTPS request to their server to fetch the audio file. We have no control over TrueMuslims.net's own privacy practices; please refer to their website for their policy.

4. Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Downloaded Quran audio files (in your device's app storage)
  • Your last-played Surah and playback position (in shared preferences)

None of this data is uploaded to any server.

5. Permissions Used

Permission Purpose
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Precise GPS coordinates for prayer times and Qibla calculation
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Fallback location for prayer times when precise GPS is unavailable
INTERNET Streaming Quran audio from TrueMuslims.net (when not downloaded)

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no server-side data at risk. Audio files stored locally on your device are protected by Android's standard app sandboxing.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
