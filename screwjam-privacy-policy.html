ScrewJam Puzzle

Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated: May 14, 2026

ScrewJam Puzzle is a free, offline puzzle game. We are committed to your privacy. This policy explains clearly what data we collect (very little) and why.

1. Information We Collect

ScrewJam Puzzle stores only the following data locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Your best star rating per puzzle level
  • Daily Challenge completion status and streak count
  • Audio and settings preferences
  • Tutorial progress

This data never leaves your device. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, or any personally identifiable information.

2. Automatically Collected Data

The app currently does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. No data is automatically transmitted to any server.

Future versions may optionally integrate Firebase Analytics (Google). If added, this policy will be updated and the analytics SDK will only collect anonymised, aggregated gameplay events (e.g. level completed, app opened). No personal data will be tied to these events.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not currently use any third-party services that collect user data. Standard device and OS features (audio, screen, storage) are used solely to run the game.

4. Children's Privacy

ScrewJam Puzzle does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). The game is suitable for all ages and contains no advertising, in-app purchases, or social features.

5. Data Storage & Security

All game progress is stored in Android's internal SharedPreferences storage, which is sandboxed to the app and inaccessible by other apps. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data.

6. Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control at all times:

  • Access & deletion — Clear app data via Android Settings → Apps → ScrewJam Puzzle → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Portability — Data exists only on your device; no account or server-side data is held.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
